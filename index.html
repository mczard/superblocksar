Super Blocks AR

Glowing glass blocks, your real room as the board. Place the well on your table or floor, or drop into a neon arena.

Mixed reality meets a neon arena

Drop glowing glass blocks into a 12 by 16 well and clear lines to score. Put the well on your real table, stand it on the floor with passthrough, or switch to a virtual neon arena. When the well overflows, a rain of glass cubes pours across your room.

How it plays

Clear lines by filling rows of the 12 by 16 well. Six odd-sized pieces, with no four-cube piece, keep every decision fresh.

The six pieces

  • Iskra1 cube
  • Igła3 cubes, a line
  • Kątownik3 cubes, a corner
  • Kolumna5 cubes, a line
  • Most5 cubes, a U
  • Flaga5 cubes, a block plus one

  • Next three

    See the next three pieces in a glass tube beside the well.

  • Hold

    Keep one piece in a hold case at the base of the well.

  • Ghost

    A glow on the floor of the well shows where the piece will land.

Clear more at once, score more

Clearing several lines at once earns a named bonus, scaled by your level.

  • 1Single line
  • 2DUET!
  • 3TERCET!
  • 4NEON!
  • 5SUPERNOVA!

Your pace

  • Calm

    Slower start and a hint to help you along.

  • Normal

    The standard challenge.

  • Fast

    Starts at a higher level.

  • Zen

    No game over; trigger the cube rain whenever you like.

A new level every 10 lines, with faster falling.

Play your way

The same game in three places. Switch between them from the pause menu without restarting your round.

  • AR Table

    Seated play with the well on your real table. Pick a surface, rotate and scale it, and the game remembers the spot.

  • AR Floor

    Stand a few steps back and play at room scale. The game-over cube rain pours across your actual room.

  • VR Neon Arena

    A fully virtual scene of glass and neon.

Controllers or hands

Play with Touch controllers: stick to move, buttons to rotate, trigger to drop, grip to hold. Or use hand gestures: thumb swipes to move, a thumb tap to rotate and a thumb swipe forward to hold. Dropping a piece with hands is not available; use the controller trigger for that.

Comfort first

Built for sessions of 3 to 15 minutes, seated or standing.

  • No locomotion

    You never move through the scene. You stay where you are.

  • Seated play

    Everything is designed to be playable seated.

  • Reduce flashes

    An option for fewer flashes. Nothing in the game flashes more than 3 times per second.

  • High contrast and symbols

    Accessibility options include high contrast and symbols on the cubes.

  • Clear of your head

    Nothing is dropped directly onto your head, and the cube rain keeps a safe zone around it.

FAQ

Which headsets does it support?

Meta Quest 3 and Meta Quest 3S.

Do I need an account?

The game has no account of its own, no ads and no analytics.

Does it use the internet?

The game has no server of its own. The only online feature is the optional Meta leaderboard: after a finished round (not Zen) your score and the name of the board, which is only the difficulty, are sent through the Meta Platform SDK built into the app. The game contacts no other server, and no room scan, camera or hand data is ever sent.

Where does my room scan go?

Nowhere. In the AR modes the game reads the room scan from the headset to place the well and, on the floor, to let cubes bounce off your room. It is processed on the headset and never sent or stored outside it. The game saves only the board position you chose, as a spatial anchor kept by the system on the headset.

Does it use the camera?

No. The game never accesses camera images and does not record video or audio. Passthrough is rendered by the system.

Is hand tracking required?

No. Hand tracking is optional and only reads thumb gestures. Controllers work for everything.

Which languages?

English and Polish.
