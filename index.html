Super Blocks AR

Glowing glass blocks, and your own room is the board. Set the well on your table or floor, or play in a neon arena.

Mixed reality meets a neon arena

Drop glowing glass blocks into a 12 by 16 well and clear lines to score. Put the well on your real table, stand it on your floor with passthrough, or switch to a virtual neon arena. When the well overflows, glass cubes rain down across your room.

How it plays

Fill a row of the 12 by 16 well and it clears. You get six pieces of one, three and five cubes, and no two feel alike.

The six pieces

  • Sparkone cube
  • Needlethree cubes in a line
  • Cornerthree cubes in a corner
  • Columnfive cubes in a line
  • Bridgefive cubes in a U
  • Flagfive cubes: a 2x2 block with one extra

  • Next three

    See the next three pieces in a glass tube beside the well.

  • Hold

    Store one piece in a display case at the base of the well.

  • Ghost

    A glow on the well floor shows where the piece will land.

Clear more at once, score more

Clear several lines at once for a bigger score and a named bonus. Your level multiplies every score.

  • 1Single line
  • 2DUET!
  • 3TERCET!
  • 4NEON!
  • 5SUPERNOVA!

Your pace

  • Calm

    A slower start and a hint to guide you.

  • Normal

    Start at the beginning and speed up as you go.

  • Fast

    Start at a higher level.

  • Zen

    You can't lose. Start the cube rain whenever you like.

Every 10 lines you reach a new level, and the blocks fall faster.

Play your way

One game, three places. Switch between them from the pause menu without restarting your round.

  • AR Table

    Sit down and play with the well on your real table. Pick a surface, turn and resize the well, and the game remembers the spot.

  • AR Floor

    Step back a few paces and play at room scale. When you lose, the cube rain pours across your actual room.

  • VR Neon Arena

    A fully virtual world of glass and neon.

Controllers or hands

With Touch controllers, the stick moves, buttons rotate, the trigger drops and the grip holds. With your hands, thumb swipes move, a thumb tap rotates and a thumb swipe forward holds. Dropping a piece still needs the controller trigger.

Comfort first

Made for sessions of 3 to 15 minutes, seated or standing.

  • No locomotion

    The scene never moves you. You stay where you are.

  • Seated play

    Designed so you can play seated.

  • Reduce flashes

    Switch on fewer flashes if you like. Nothing in the game flashes more than 3 times per second.

  • High contrast and symbols

    Turn on high contrast and symbols on the cubes in the accessibility options.

  • Nothing falls on your head

    No piece ever falls straight onto your head, and the cube rain keeps a safe zone around it.

FAQ

Which headsets does it run on?

Meta Quest 3 and Meta Quest 3S.

Do I need an account?

No. The game has no account of its own, no ads and no analytics.

Does it use the internet?

Only for the optional Meta leaderboard. The game has no server of its own. After a finished round (not Zen), your score and the name of the board, which is only the difficulty, are sent through the Meta Platform SDK built into the app. The game contacts no other server, and no room scan, camera or hand data is ever sent.

Where does my room scan go?

Nowhere. In the AR modes the game reads the room scan from the headset to place the well and, on the floor, to let cubes bounce off your room. The scan is processed on the headset and never sent or stored outside it. The game saves only the board position you chose, as a spatial anchor kept by the system on the headset.

Does it use the camera?

No. The game never accesses camera images and does not record video or audio. The system renders passthrough.

Is hand tracking required?

No. Hand tracking is optional and reads only thumb gestures. Controllers do everything.

Which languages?

English and Polish.
